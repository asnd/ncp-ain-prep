## When discounts tend to appear

The verified 2026 pattern is:

- Certification launch/preparation webinars: January and April offered attendee-specific 50% codes.
- GTC conferences: on-site certification exams may be included at no extra charge; GTC Berlin is the next confirmed opportunity on October 20–22, 2026.
- NVIDIA training announcements: subscribe through [NVIDIA Academy Support](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/training/academy/support/) or the training newsletter. NVIDIA says it does not have a standing student or need-based discount.

This is an observed pattern, not a promise that NVIDIA will repeat a promotion.

## Redemption and verification

NVIDIA says certification vouchers and promo codes are entered in the purchase flow. Before booking:

1. Confirm the code applies to **NCP-AIN** or **NCP-AII**, not only associate or generative-AI exams.
2. Check both the purchase deadline and the deadline to sit the exam; they may differ.
3. Confirm region, currency, single-use restrictions, and whether the voucher is tied to the webinar registration email.
4. Do not complete payment until the discounted total is visible.
5. If an attendee voucher is missing, use NVIDIA Academy support and include the event registration email.

## Offers intentionally excluded

- Influencer and social-media codes whose current validity cannot be confirmed by NVIDIA.
- “Exam dump” sites and practice-test coupons; these are not NVIDIA exam discounts and may violate exam rules.
- Expired year-end promotions.
