# Udemy resources for NCP-AIN and NCP-AII

Verified: 2026-09-10

This is a dated inventory of Udemy listings found for the two exams. Every listing marked **available** was opened individually and returned a live Udemy course page during verification. A live page confirms availability, not technical accuracy, teaching quality, or NVIDIA endorsement. Titles are reproduced exactly as listed, including spelling errors.

Udemy prices are omitted because they vary by account, promotion, and region. Ratings, enrollment, course contents, and availability can also change. Check the course page immediately before purchasing.

## Quick assessment

- None of the verified listings is an official NVIDIA course.
- Most are practice-question banks rather than taught courses or hands-on labs.
- One NCP-AIN listing has substantial lecture content, but it uses simulated exercises and contains exam-information discrepancies.
- One NCP-AII listing has lecture content, but much of its syllabus is outside the current exam blueprint and its listed “labs” are only seconds long.
- No verified Udemy listing replaces DSX Air, UFM/InfiniBand practice, the Base Command Manager VM lab, or access to physical Spectrum, DGX/HGX, BlueField, and multi-GPU systems.

Use the [official NCP-AIN study guide](ncp-ain-prep.md) or [official NCP-AII study guide](ncp-aii/README.md) as the source of truth. Treat Udemy material as optional review and cross-check disputed answers against current NVIDIA documentation.

## NCP-AIN taught course

### [NCP-AIN: NVIDIA AI Networking Certification Prep](https://www.udemy.com/course/ncp-ain-nvidia-ai-networking-certification-prep/)

- **Availability:** Available; live page verified 2026-09-10
- **Instructor:** Aseem Mankotia
- **Listing metadata:** Updated August 2026; English; 12 sections and 12 lectures
- **Coverage shown:** rail-optimized and fat-tree design, UFM, adaptive routing, SHARP, PKeys, QoS, InfiniBand tools, Spectrum-X, RoCE, ECN/PFC, NVUE, Ansible, WJH, DOCA, and Kubernetes RDMA
- **Lab reality:** The listing says no physical lab is required and describes simulated exercises using diagrams, configuration snippets, command output, and WJH examples. This is not a hosted hands-on fabric.
- **Assessment:** Moderate blueprint fit as a compact overview. The listing says NVIDIA does not publish per-domain weights, although the current official exam page does, and describes an approximate 60–70-question exam while NVIDIA currently lists 70–75. The listing also discloses AI assistance and AI voice. Verify all exam facts and technical details independently.

- **Availability:** Available; live page verified 2026-09-10

- **Listing metadata:** Updated February 2026; English; 11 sections, 51 lectures, 3 hours 6 minutes
- **Coverage shown:** infrastructure concepts, GPUs, Kubernetes, Triton, TensorRT, edge/Jetson, security, and compliance
- **Lab reality:** The page lists lab items lasting roughly 5–13 seconds. These appear to be lab prompts or descriptions, not hosted hands-on labs. The requirements mention access to A100/H100 or similar hardware.

## How to use third-party question banks safely

1. Finish the relevant official study-guide domain before using questions from that domain.
2. Use one question bank for gap detection rather than memorizing answer wording.
3. Look up every uncertain or disputed answer in current NVIDIA documentation.
4. Reject questions that conflict with the current exam blueprint, current product behavior, or supported commands.
5. Do not use products claiming leaked, actual, or guaranteed exam questions. Exam dumps are neither reliable preparation nor appropriate exam conduct.
